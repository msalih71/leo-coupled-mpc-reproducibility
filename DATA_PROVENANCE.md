# Data provenance

Closed-loop trajectories are numerical outputs, not observed spacecraft control records. All recorded campaign outcomes, including failed qualifications, are preserved in JSON/CSV. Selected computed NPZ/NPY traces are included; omitted full trajectories are regenerated by the full pipeline.

- `data/SW-All_observed.csv`: conversion of CelesTrak SW-All's observed section from the pinned `spaceweather` package, snapshot updated 21 July 2025. Rebuilt by `code/sw_data.py`. Source: https://celestrak.org/SpaceData/ . GFZ Kp/ap attribution: Matzka et al. (2021), DOI 10.1029/2020SW002641.
- NRLMSISE-00 and NRLMSIS2.1: evaluated using `pymsis`; see manuscript references and package terms.
- Swarm: ESA Level2 POD/ACC retrieved densities from https://vires.services/hapi/ . Committed tables are derived analyses. Raw downloaded densities are excluded; follow provider terms.
- JB2008: unmodified SET code and historical indices from https://spacewx.com/jb2008/ . Vendor code/indices are excluded. `external_validation/README.md` specifies compilation and driver cross-checks.

External tables reflect source files available on 24 September 2026. Later provider changes may prevent byte-identical external reconstruction. The bundled simulation inputs remain fixed.

Swarm and simulation orbits differ. Causal-input withholding is retrospective emulation, not a recorded uplink. Power assumptions and sensitivity ranges are numerical choices; no flight-processor benchmark or formal stability guarantee is supplied. Protocols and manuscript state the limits.
